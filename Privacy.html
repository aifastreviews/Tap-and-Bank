1Information We Collect

We collect only limited information. This may include information you choose to send us (such as your name and email address when you contact support) and technical information collected automatically, such as browser type, device type, pages visited, referring page and approximate location derived from your IP address.

We do not collect payment card details. Purchases are completed on the JVZoo (referenced in the supplied promotional page; confirm the actual checkout provider before purchase) checkout page, which is operated by a third party.

3Cookies & Tracking Technologies

This website and the third-party services it links to may use cookies and similar technologies. Affiliate links use tracking cookies set by JVZoo (referenced in the supplied promotional page; confirm the actual checkout provider before purchase) so that a purchase can be attributed to the referring affiliate. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies will not prevent you from reading this website, but may affect affiliate attribution.

We share information only where needed to run this website or where required by law, for example with hosting providers, analytics providers and affiliate networks. When you click an order button you are taken to JVZoo (referenced in the supplied promotional page; confirm the actual checkout provider before purchase) and the product vendor (Jonas Lindgren), whose own privacy policies govern the information you provide to them.

We never sell your data, and we do not share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing.

We keep personal information only as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this policy, such as resolving a support request, or as long as the law requires. When it is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@tapandbank.us. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.
